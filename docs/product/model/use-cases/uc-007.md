## Goal

Record a payment from one participant to another.

## Trigger

User selects "Record payment" from the group screen, optionally pre-filled from a settlement suggestion.

## Preconditions

The group is not archived. Both participants exist in the group. Sender and receiver are different.

## Main Flow

1. User selects who is paying and who is receiving.
2. User enters the amount.
3. System records the transfer.

## Postconditions

## Alternative Flows

- User records a payment that does not match any settlement suggestion.
- User records a partial payment amount.

## Failure Conditions

- The group is archived.
- Sender and receiver are the same participant.

## Postconditions

A new manual transfer exists in the group. Balances are updated.
