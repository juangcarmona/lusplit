  - A split definition with one or more components (fixed, equal, weighted, or percentage)

  - The sum of all allocated shares equals the expense amount exactly
  - No minor units are left unassigned

## Intent

Make concrete the full-consumption invariant: every minor unit of the expense amount must be allocated to a participant share after split evaluation.

## Boundaries

This behaviour covers the allocation result, not the validation of individual split components (e.g., percentage sum must be 100 — see BR-006). A split that leaves a remainder is rejected before the expense is recorded.
