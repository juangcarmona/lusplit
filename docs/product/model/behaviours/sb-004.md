## Intent

Make concrete the zero-sum invariant: every minor unit paid into the group must be owed by someone, so the algebraic sum of all balances is always zero.

## Boundaries

This behaviour covers the per-participant balance calculation. Household-aggregated balances (BR-009) also preserve the zero-sum property but at the economic-unit level. A non-zero sum indicates a calculation error or data corruption, not a valid state.
