## Intent

Make concrete the household aggregation rule: dependents (e.g., children) do not settle debts independently; their responsible adult's balance absorbs the dependent's balance for household-level settlement.

## Boundaries

This behaviour covers the aggregation view only. Per-participant balances remain available (UC-008) and still satisfy the zero-sum invariant (BR-008). An owner who is not a member of their own economic unit is a data integrity error, not a valid state.
