## Intent

Make concrete the immutability guarantee of an archived group: no mutation use case may succeed against a closed group, while read access is preserved.

## Boundaries

This behaviour covers mutation rejection only. Archiving itself (UC-011) is idempotent — re-archiving an already-archived group succeeds without error. Exporting and viewing archived data are read operations and are not blocked.
