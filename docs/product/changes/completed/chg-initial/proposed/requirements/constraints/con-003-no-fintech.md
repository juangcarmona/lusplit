## Constraint

The product does not include budgeting workflows, banking features, or fintech positioning.

## Rationale

The product is a calm utility for shared expenses, not a financial management tool. Adding budgeting or banking features would change the product's identity and increase regulatory considerations.

## Consequences

No spending limits, no budget categories, no bank integrations, no investment tracking. The product focuses solely on splitting and settling shared costs.
